Neighbourhood guide

Braamfontein

Braamfontein sits just north of the city centre, between the University of the Witwatersrand and Constitution Hill. It is young, busy and easy to explore on foot by day.

{{ info_box }}

What to Do

Start at Constitution Hill, the former prison that is now home to the Constitutional Court. Then walk west into the main streets of Braamfontein for coffee, galleries and shops run by local designers.

On Saturdays, a food market in Juta Street draws a big crowd. Arrive before midday for the best choice. In the evening, rooftop bars and restaurants fill up with students and people who work nearby.

Getting There

Braamfontein is a short ride-hailing trip from Rosebank or the city centre. Park Station, on the Gautrain line, is about a 15-minute walk away. The Nelson Mandela Bridge links Braamfontein with Newtown to the south.

Staying Safe

The main streets are busy and well used in the day. After dark, use ride-hailing rather than walking between venues, and keep your phone out of sight on the street.
