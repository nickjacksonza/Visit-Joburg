Tips for Your Visit

  • Many museums close on Mondays. Check before you plan your week.
  • The Apartheid Museum and Gold Reef City are next to each other, so you can visit both on one day.
  • Soweto is best with a local guide. Walking and cycling tours start near Vilakazi Street.
  • Leave a full day for the Cradle of Humankind. You need a car or a tour to get there.
