# Johannesburg Neighbourhoods to Explore

Joburg is spread out, so it helps to think of it as a set of smaller places. Pick one or two areas a day and travel between them by Gautrain or ride-hailing.

## Where to Go

### Braamfontein

A student district next to Wits University. Rooftop bars, galleries, coffee shops and a busy Saturday food market.

Good for: Food markets, nightlife, Constitution Hill

[Open in Maps: Braamfontein](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=-26.1929,28.0357)

### Maboneng

A small, walkable precinct on the east side of the city centre, known for street art, galleries, independent shops and restaurants.

Good for: Street art, galleries, weekend markets

[Open in Maps: Maboneng](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=-26.2044,28.0583)

### Soweto

South Africa's best-known township and the heart of much of the country's history. Go with a local guide to get the most from it.

Good for: History, Vilakazi Street, local food

[Open in Maps: Soweto](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=-26.2485,27.854)

### Melville

A relaxed suburb with a strip of cafés, bookshops and bars along 7th Street, close to the Melville Koppies nature reserve.

Good for: Cafés, live music, a walk on the koppies

[Open in Maps: Melville](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=-26.175,28.008)

### Rosebank

A compact business and shopping district with its own Gautrain station, a weekend rooftop market and several galleries.

Good for: A first base, shopping, galleries

[Open in Maps: Rosebank](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=-26.146,28.0436)

### Newtown

A cultural quarter in the west of the city centre, home to the Market Theatre and Mary Fitzgerald Square.

Good for: Theatre, music, history

[Open in Maps: Newtown](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=-26.203,28.032)

## Where to Stay

For a first visit, stay in Rosebank or Sandton. Both have Gautrain stations, a good choice of hotels and restaurants within a short walk. Melville and Parkhurst have smaller guest houses with a quieter, local feel.
