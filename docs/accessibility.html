Accessibility

Accessibility Statement

We aim to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA on every page of this site.

What We Do

  • One main heading per page and headings in order.
  • Landmarks for the header, navigation, main content and footer, and a skip link as the first thing in the tab order.
  • Text contrast of at least 4.5 to 1 in both light and dark colour schemes.
  • Visible focus on every link and control, and touch targets of at least 44 by 44 pixels.
  • A text alternative for every image that carries meaning.
  • Form fields with visible labels.
  • No movement unless you ask for it, and support for reduced motion and forced colours.
  • A menu that works with the keyboard. The Escape key closes it.

How We Check

Every build runs automated checks on headings, images, links, forms and colour contrast. Before each release we also test with Lighthouse, axe and a screen reader.
