Transport

Gautrain Guide for Visitors

The Gautrain is the rapid rail line that links O.R. Tambo International Airport, Sandton, Rosebank, Park Station in the city centre, Midrand and Pretoria. It is the quickest way to cross the north of Johannesburg, and the easiest way to leave the airport.

The Two Lines

The Gautrain has two lines. They meet at Marlboro station, just east of Sandton.

  • North-South line: Park Station, Rosebank, Sandton, Marlboro, Midrand, Centurion, Pretoria and Hatfield.
  • Airport line: Sandton, Marlboro, Rhodesfield and O.R. Tambo International Airport.

The orange line on our map of Johannesburg shows both lines. It joins the stations in order and does not follow the exact track.

Stations Visitors Use Most

O.R. Tambo International Airport

The station is in the Central Terminal Building. The trip to Sandton takes about 15 minutes. Most visitors take this train on arrival and a ride-hailing car for the last stretch to their hotel.

Sandton

Sandton station is on the corner of Rivonia Road and West Street, beside Sandton's shopping centres. Sandton City and Nelson Mandela Square are a short walk away. This is where most people change trains between the airport and the rest of the city.

Rosebank

Rosebank station is on Oxford Road at the corner of Baker Street, in the middle of the shops and the weekend rooftop market. It is also the best station for the Parks: Parkhurst, Parkview and Parktown are a short ride-hailing trip away.

Park Station

Park Station is in the city centre. From here you can reach Braamfontein and Constitution Hill by a short walk or ride. Take care in the streets around the station and use ride-hailing after dark.

Midrand, Centurion, Pretoria and Hatfield

These stations serve the north of the Johannesburg area and Pretoria. They are further than most first-time visitors go, but they are the way to reach Pretoria for a day trip.

Tickets and Cards

You pay at the station gates. The Gautrain website explains the cards and ways to pay, and lists current fares. Fares change, so we do not list them here.

Timetable

The Gautrain changes its timetable from time to time. Check the latest version on the official Gautrain website before you travel, and again on the day if you have a flight to catch. Allow extra time to reach the airport.

Getting From the Station to Your Destination

Gautrain buses connect stations with nearby suburbs, and ride-hailing cars wait outside the main stations. Routes and stops change, so check the Gautrain website for the current bus routes.
