Neighbourhoods

Johannesburg Neighbourhoods to Explore

Joburg is spread out, so it helps to think of it as a set of smaller places. Pick one or two areas a day and travel between them by Gautrain or ride-hailing. The map on the home page shows where each one sits.

Where to Go

Lonehill

A residential suburb in the far north of the city, next to Fourways. We list no visitor sights here yet; Fourways' shopping centres are a short drive away.

Open in Maps: Lonehill

Fourways

A busy suburb in the far north of the city, known for large shopping centres and the Montecasino entertainment complex.

Good for: Shopping, theatre and a family day out

Read the Fourways guide · Open in Maps: Fourways

Glenferness

A residential area in the far north of the city. We list no visitor sights here yet.

Halfway House

A suburb in Midrand, named for its position roughly halfway between Johannesburg and Pretoria. Mostly offices, homes and shops.

Open in Maps: Halfway House

Glen Austin

A semi-rural area of smallholdings in Midrand, in the far north of the city. We list no visitor sights here yet.

Open in Maps: Glen Austin

Jukskei Park

A residential suburb in the north-west of the city, named after the Jukskei River. We list no visitor sights here yet.

Open in Maps: Jukskei Park

Douglasdale

A residential suburb in the north of the city, between Sandton and Fourways. We list no visitor sights here yet.

Open in Maps: Douglasdale

Olivedale

A residential suburb in the north-west of the city, near Randburg. We list no visitor sights here yet.

Open in Maps: Olivedale

Randpark Ridge

A residential suburb in the north-west of the city, near Randburg. We list no visitor sights here yet.

Open in Maps: Randpark Ridge

Ferndale

A residential suburb next to the Randburg town centre, in the north-west of the city. We list no visitor sights here yet.

Open in Maps: Ferndale

Fairland

A residential suburb in the north-west of the city, near Randburg and Northcliff. We list no visitor sights here yet.

Open in Maps: Fairland

Robindale

A residential suburb in the north-west of the city, near Randburg. We list no visitor sights here yet.

Open in Maps: Robindale

Linden

A residential suburb in the north-west of the city with a choice of restaurants and cafés. It is a short drive from Melville and Parkhurst.

Good for: Restaurants and cafés

Open in Maps: Linden

Northcliff

A suburb on and around Northcliff Hill, one of the highest points in the city. Quiet, leafy streets with views over the northern suburbs.

Good for: Views and quiet streets

Open in Maps: Northcliff

Emmarentia

A leafy suburb around Emmarentia Dam, where people walk, run and sail. The Johannesburg Botanical Garden is on the dam's edge.

Good for: Walks, picnics and the Botanical Garden

Open in Maps: Emmarentia

Florida

A suburb in the north-west of the city, close to Roodepoort, built around Florida Lake. Mostly homes and shops.

Open in Maps: Florida

Triomf

Built in the 1950s on the site of Sophiatown, whose residents were forced to leave under apartheid. The name Sophiatown was restored in 2006.

Good for: Apartheid-era history

Open in Maps: Triomf

Melville

A relaxed suburb with a strip of cafés, bookshops and bars along 7th Street, close to the Melville Koppies nature reserve.

Good for: Cafés, live music, a walk on the koppies

Open in Maps: Melville

Sandton

The business and shopping heart of northern Johannesburg. Sandton City, Nelson Mandela Square, many hotels and a Gautrain station sit close together.

Good for: Shopping, hotels and a Gautrain base

Read the Sandton guide

Bryanston

An established northern suburb with large gardens, a shopping centre and a well-known organic market. A short drive from Sandton.

Good for: A market morning and a quiet meal

Open in Maps: Bryanston

Rivonia

A northern suburb known for Liliesleaf, the farm where anti-apartheid leaders were arrested in 1963 and which is now a museum. Also home to offices and restaurants.

Good for: Liliesleaf and the Rivonia Trial story

Open in Maps: Rivonia

Woodmead

A business district on the N1 north of Sandton, with offices and retail parks. Most visitors pass through on the way to somewhere else.

Open in Maps: Woodmead

Morningside

A residential suburb on the edge of Sandton, with a shopping centre and a number of restaurants. It lies close to the Sandton Gautrain station.

Good for: Restaurants near Sandton

Open in Maps: Morningside

River Club

A small suburb in the Sandton area, in the north of the city. We list no visitor sights here yet.

Open in Maps: River Club

Sandown

The part of Sandton around Sandton City and Nelson Mandela Square. Shops, restaurants, hotels and the Sandton Gautrain station are all within walking distance.

Good for: Shopping, hotels and a Gautrain station

Open in Maps: Sandown

Buccleuch

A suburb beside the N1 in the north of the city, with offices and light industry. We list no visitor sights here yet.

Open in Maps: Buccleuch

Modderfontein

Once the site of a dynamite factory founded in the 1890s. Today it mixes new housing and offices with the Modderfontein Reserve, which has walking trails.

Good for: A walk in the reserve

Open in Maps: Modderfontein

Kelvin

A mostly industrial and commercial suburb on the north-eastern side of the city. We list no visitor sights here yet.

Open in Maps: Kelvin

Bordeaux

A residential suburb in the north-west of the city, close to Randburg and Craighall. We list no visitor sights here yet.

Open in Maps: Bordeaux

Parkmore

A suburb just west of Sandton's business centre, with offices, apartment blocks and houses. It is one of the Parks and also counted as part of Sandton.

Open in Maps: Parkmore

Hurlingham

A residential suburb in the Sandton area, in the north of the city. We list no visitor sights here yet.

Open in Maps: Hurlingham

Sandhurst

One of the city's wealthiest residential suburbs, close to Sandton's centre. Large houses sit behind high walls, so it is a place to drive through rather than visit.

Open in Maps: Sandhurst

Wynberg

A mostly industrial and commercial suburb next to Alexandra, on the north-eastern side of the city. We list no visitor sights here yet.

Open in Maps: Wynberg

Alexandra

A dense township founded in 1912, just east of Sandton. Nelson Mandela lived here in the 1940s. Go with a local guide.

Good for: Local history, with a guide

Open in Maps: Alexandra

Hyde Park

A wealthy residential suburb best known for Hyde Park Corner, a shopping centre with fashion and homeware shops, restaurants and cafés.

Good for: Shopping and a coffee

Open in Maps: Hyde Park

The Parks

A local name for a group of leafy suburbs north of the city centre whose names start with Park, from Parkhurst and Parkview to Parktown.

Good for: Restaurants, Zoo Lake and old houses

Read the The Parks guide

Parkhurst

A residential suburb known for 4th Avenue, a street of restaurants, cafés and independent shops. One of the Parks.

Good for: Restaurants and browsing on 4th Avenue

Open in Maps: Parkhurst

Parktown North

A residential suburb with a mix of houses and offices, north of Parkwood and Parkhurst. One of the Parks.

Open in Maps: Parktown North

Parkwood

A quiet residential suburb close to Rosebank, between Parkhurst and Parktown North. One of the Parks.

Open in Maps: Parkwood

Parkview

A leafy residential suburb beside Zoo Lake, with a golf course and a small strip of shops and restaurants on Tyrone Avenue. One of the Parks.

Good for: Zoo Lake, picnics and a quiet lunch

Open in Maps: Parkview

Parktown

A suburb on a ridge north of Braamfontein, known for its old houses, many designed by Herbert Baker, and for several large hospitals. One of the Parks.

Good for: Old houses and city views

Open in Maps: Parktown

Illovo

A suburb between Sandton and Rosebank along Oxford Road, with offices, apartment blocks and restaurants.

Good for: Restaurants and a base near both

Open in Maps: Illovo

Melrose

An upmarket suburb best known for Melrose Arch, a walkable precinct of restaurants, shops, offices and hotels.

Good for: A walkable evening out

Open in Maps: Melrose

Rosebank

A compact business and shopping district with its own Gautrain station, a weekend rooftop market and several galleries.

Good for: A first base, shopping, galleries

Open in Maps: Rosebank

Saxonwold

A leafy suburb of old houses and large gardens, home to the Johannesburg Zoo. Zoo Lake is next door.

Good for: The Johannesburg Zoo

Open in Maps: Saxonwold

Orchards

A residential suburb in the north-east of the city. We list no visitor sights here yet.

Open in Maps: Orchards

Sandringham

A residential suburb in the north-east of the city. We list no visitor sights here yet.

Open in Maps: Sandringham

Edenvale

A town just east of Johannesburg, in the Ekurhuleni municipality, close to OR Tambo International Airport. Mostly homes, shops and light industry.

Open in Maps: Edenvale

Houghton

A leafy, wealthy suburb where Nelson Mandela lived for many years and died in 2013. The Nelson Mandela Foundation has its offices here. The Mandela home is private.

Good for: Mandela history, from the street

Open in Maps: Houghton

Observatory

A residential suburb on a ridge east of the city centre, named after the observatory built there in the early 1900s. Observatory Park is in the suburb.

Open in Maps: Observatory

Bruma

A suburb in the east of the city, centred on Bruma Lake. Mostly shops, offices and apartment blocks.

Open in Maps: Bruma

Bedfordview

A suburb just east of Johannesburg on the N3, in the Ekurhuleni municipality, with the Eastgate shopping centre and a number of restaurants.

Good for: Shopping and restaurants on the way to the airport

Open in Maps: Bedfordview

Braamfontein

A student district next to Wits University. Rooftop bars, galleries, coffee shops and a busy Saturday food market.

Good for: Food markets, nightlife, Constitution Hill

Read the Braamfontein guide · Open in Maps: Braamfontein

Newtown

A cultural quarter in the west of the city centre, home to the Market Theatre and Mary Fitzgerald Square.

Good for: Theatre, music, history

Open in Maps: Newtown

Yeoville

A busy, mixed suburb east of Hillbrow. Rockey Street was known for its bars and live music, and the area has long been home to many people from other African countries.

Good for: Local history and a lively street

Open in Maps: Yeoville

Maboneng

A small, walkable precinct on the east side of the city centre, known for street art, galleries, independent shops and restaurants.

Good for: Street art, galleries, weekend markets

Open in Maps: Maboneng

Kensington

A residential suburb in the east of the city. We list no visitor sights here yet.

Open in Maps: Kensington

Auckland Park

A suburb in the west of the city, home to the Kingsway campus of the University of Johannesburg and the main studios of the SABC.

Open in Maps: Auckland Park

Riverlea

A residential suburb in the west of the city. We list no visitor sights here yet.

Open in Maps: Riverlea

Robertsham

A residential suburb in the south of the city. We list no visitor sights here yet.

Open in Maps: Robertsham

Kenilworth

A residential suburb in the south of the city. We list no visitor sights here yet.

Open in Maps: Kenilworth

Meredale

A suburb in the south of the city, home to the Southgate shopping centre.

Good for: Shopping

Open in Maps: Meredale

Glenvista

A residential suburb in the south of the city. We list no visitor sights here yet.

Open in Maps: Glenvista

Soweto

South Africa's best-known township and the heart of much of the country's history. Go with a local guide to get the most from it.

Good for: History, Vilakazi Street, local food

Orlando West

The part of Soweto that most visitors see first. Vilakazi Street, Mandela House and the Hector Pieterson Memorial and Museum are all within a short walk of each other.

Good for: Mandela House, Vilakazi Street and the 1976 uprising

Open in Maps: Orlando West

Diepkloof

A township in Soweto, home to the Chris Hani Baragwanath Hospital. We list no other visitor sights here yet.

Open in Maps: Diepkloof

Hartbeespoort

A dam and holiday town about an hour's drive north-west of the city, in North West province. Popular for weekend trips from Johannesburg.

Good for: A day out of the city

Open in Maps: Hartbeespoort

Where to Stay

For a first visit, stay in Rosebank or Sandton. Both have Gautrain stations, a good choice of hotels and restaurants within a short walk. Melville and Parkhurst have smaller guest houses with a quieter, local feel.
