# How to Spend Your First Day in Johannesburg

By the Visit Joburg team. Published 11 November 2026.

A first day in Johannesburg can feel like a lot. This plan keeps travel short, puts the history first and ends somewhere you can relax. It works best with a ride-hailing app or a booked driver.

## Morning: The Apartheid Museum

Arrive when the museum opens. The exhibits move from the start of apartheid to the first democratic election in 1994, and they take time to read and watch. Two to three hours is about right. Your entry ticket tells you which entrance to use, based on a racial category, so the visit starts with how apartheid divided people.

## Lunch: Vilakazi Street, Soweto

Take a short ride west to Soweto. Vilakazi Street has several restaurants serving South African food, from braaied meat to pap and chakalaka. After lunch, visit Mandela House, then walk to the Hector Pieterson Memorial. A local guide adds the stories you will not find on the signs.

## Afternoon: Constitution Hill

Head back towards the city centre for Constitution Hill. The old prison cells sit next to the Constitutional Court, which was built partly from the bricks of the demolished awaiting-trial block. Join a guided tour if you can.

## Evening: Braamfontein or Maboneng

Constitution Hill sits on the edge of Braamfontein, so you can end the day at a rooftop bar or a restaurant a few minutes away. If you prefer street art and small galleries, take a short ride to Maboneng instead. Either way, book your ride home rather than walking after dark.

## Before You Go

- Check opening days. Many museums close on Mondays.
- Carry a little cash for tips and small purchases in Soweto.
- Wear comfortable shoes and bring a jersey for the evening.
