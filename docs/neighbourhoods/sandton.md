# Sandton

Sandton is the business and shopping district of northern Johannesburg. It is the part of the city with the most hotels, offices and large shopping centres, and it is the easiest place to reach from the airport by train.

## Sandton at a Glance

The business and shopping heart of northern Johannesburg. Sandton City, Nelson Mandela Square, many hotels and a Gautrain station sit close together.

**Part of the city**

Sandton, Rosebank and North Central

**Good for**

Shopping, Food

## What to Do

Most visitors start at Sandton City, a large shopping centre opened in 1973. A walkway leads from it to Nelson Mandela Square, where a bronze statue of Nelson Mandela stands among restaurants and cafés. Both are easy to reach on foot from the Sandton Gautrain station.

Sandton is a shopping and dining area more than a sightseeing one. For museums and history, plan day trips to [Braamfontein](https://example.com/neighbourhoods/braamfontein.html) or the Apartheid Museum, listed on the [things to do](https://example.com/things-to-do.html) page.

People use "Sandton" for a wider group of suburbs than the business district. The ones below are the most useful to know. Areas around the centre are quiet and residential, so expect to travel by car or ride-hailing.

## Sandhurst

### Sandhurst at a Glance

One of the city's wealthiest residential suburbs, close to Sandton's centre. Large houses sit behind high walls, so it is a place to drive through rather than visit.

## Getting There

The [Gautrain](https://example.com/gautrain.html) runs from OR Tambo International Airport to Sandton station in about 15 minutes. From there, Rosebank is one stop away and Park Station, in the city centre, is a longer ride on the same line. Check the current timetable and fares on the Gautrain website before you travel.

Outside the centre, distances are larger than they look on a map. Use ride-hailing or a hotel transfer, and allow extra time at rush hour.

## Staying Safe

The area around Sandton City and Nelson Mandela Square is busy and well guarded in the day. In the evening, book a ride-hailing car to the door rather than walking between venues, and keep phones and bags out of sight on the street.
