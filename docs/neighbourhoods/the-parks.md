# The Parks

"The Parks" is a local name for a group of suburbs north of the city centre whose names start with Park: Parkhurst, Parkview, Parktown, Parkwood, Parktown North and Parkmore. They are leafy, mostly residential and close to Rosebank.

## The Parks at a Glance

A local name for a group of leafy suburbs north of the city centre whose names start with Park, from Parkhurst and Parkview to Parktown.

**Part of the city**

Sandton, Rosebank and North Central

**Good for**

Food, Outdoors

## What to Do

Each suburb has a different character, so pick one or two for a day. Parkhurst has a street of restaurants and shops, Parkview has Zoo Lake, and Parktown has some of the city's oldest houses.

## Parkhurst

Walk 4th Avenue for coffee, lunch and independent shops. It is the busiest of the Parks in the evenings.

### Parkhurst at a Glance

A residential suburb known for 4th Avenue, a street of restaurants, cafés and independent shops. One of the Parks.

**Part of the city**

Sandton, Rosebank and North Central

**Good for**

## Parkview

Zoo Lake is the main draw. Bring a picnic, hire a rowing boat, or walk around the lake. A small strip of restaurants on Tyrone Avenue is a short drive away.

### Parkview at a Glance

A leafy residential suburb beside Zoo Lake, with a golf course and a small strip of shops and restaurants on Tyrone Avenue. One of the Parks.

## Parktown

Parktown sits on a ridge, and many of its old houses were designed by the architect Herbert Baker. Most are private, so look from the street and respect residents. The area also has several large hospitals, so traffic is heavy on weekdays.

### Parktown at a Glance

A suburb on a ridge north of Braamfontein, known for its old houses, many designed by Herbert Baker, and for several large hospitals. One of the Parks.

**Part of the city**

Sandton, Rosebank and North Central

**Good for**

History, Architecture

## Parkwood, Parktown North and Parkmore

These three are mostly homes and offices. Parkwood and Parktown North lie close to Rosebank, which makes them handy places to stay. Parkmore is also counted as part of [Sandton](https://example.com/neighbourhoods/sandton.html).

## Getting There

The Parks do not have their own Gautrain stations. Use Rosebank station and take a short ride-hailing trip, or travel by car. Distances between the suburbs are short, but the roads are busy at rush hour.

## Staying Safe

The streets are quiet and the main shopping streets are busy. Keep valuables out of sight, park in guarded or well-lit places, and use ride-hailing after dark.
