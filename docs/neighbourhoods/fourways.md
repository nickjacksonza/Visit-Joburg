# Fourways

Fourways is a large suburb in the far north of Johannesburg. Most visitors come for its shopping centres and for Montecasino, an entertainment complex with theatres, restaurants and a casino.

## Fourways at a Glance

A busy suburb in the far north of the city, known for large shopping centres and the Montecasino entertainment complex.

**Part of the city**

Far North and Midrand

**Good for**

Shopping, Entertainment, Family

**Places here**

- Cedar Square
- Fourways Mall
- Montecasino

## What to Do

Montecasino is built to look like an Italian village, with streets, squares and restaurants under one roof. It has theatres that stage musicals and shows, cinemas and a bird garden that suits a family outing.

Fourways Mall is one of the largest shopping centres in South Africa. Cedar Square is smaller and quicker to get around. Both have restaurants for a meal between shops.

## Getting There

Fourways has no Gautrain station, so most visitors arrive by car or ride-hailing. From Sandton, the trip takes about 20 to 40 minutes, depending on traffic. Allow extra time at rush hour.

## Nearby

Lonehill, Dainfern and Kyalami are close by. Further south, Bryanston and Sandton have more hotels and restaurants.
