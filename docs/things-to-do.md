# Things to Do in Johannesburg

These are the places we suggest for a first visit. Hours and prices change, so check each place's own website before you go.

## Museums, Landmarks and Day Trips

### Apartheid Museum

Ormonde

The story of apartheid and the struggle against it, told through film, photographs and personal accounts. Allow at least two to three hours.

- History
- Museum

[Open in Maps: Apartheid Museum](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=-26.2375,28.0103)

### Constitution Hill

Braamfontein

A former prison complex where Nelson Mandela and Mahatma Gandhi were held, now home to the Constitutional Court. Guided tours run through the day.

- History
- Architecture

[Open in Maps: Constitution Hill](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=-26.1889,28.0425)

### Vilakazi Street, Soweto

Orlando West, Soweto

The only street in the world where two Nobel Peace Prize winners lived: Nelson Mandela and Archbishop Desmond Tutu. Visit Mandela House and eat at one of the street's restaurants.

- History
- Food

[Open in Maps: Vilakazi Street, Soweto](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=-26.2385,27.9081)

### Hector Pieterson Memorial and Museum

Orlando West, Soweto

A memorial to the students killed in the Soweto uprising of 16 June 1976, a short walk from Vilakazi Street.

- History
- Museum

[Open in Maps: Hector Pieterson Memorial and Museum](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=-26.2381,27.9095)

### Cradle of Humankind

About 50 km northwest of the city

A UNESCO World Heritage Site with some of the world's richest fossil finds of early humans. Visit the Maropeng visitor centre and the Sterkfontein Caves. Plan a full day.

- Day trip
- Science
- Outdoors

[Open in Maps: Cradle of Humankind](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=-25.9667,27.6622)

### Johannesburg Botanical Garden

Emmarentia

A large public garden with a rose garden, herb garden and walking paths beside Emmarentia Dam. Free to enter and good for a picnic.

- Outdoors
- Free

[Open in Maps: Johannesburg Botanical Garden](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=-26.1597,28.0028)

### Origins Centre

Braamfontein

A museum at Wits University about the origins of modern humans, with a large collection of southern African rock art.

- Science
- Museum

[Open in Maps: Origins Centre](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=-26.1925,28.03)

### Liliesleaf

Rivonia

The farm that served as a secret base for the liberation movement in the 1960s. The 1963 police raid here led to the Rivonia Trial.

- History
- Museum

[Open in Maps: Liliesleaf](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=-26.0442,28.0569)

## Tips for Your Visit

- Many museums close on Mondays. Check before you plan your week.
- The Apartheid Museum and Gold Reef City are next to each other, so you can visit both on one day.
- Soweto is best with a local guide. Walking and cycling tours start near Vilakazi Street.
- Leave a full day for the Cradle of Humankind. You need a car or a tour to get there.
